# Native entrance headroom specimen a001

The proposed dimensions pass this scoped headroom study without correction. This is an editable Blender entrance specimen, not a complete stair kit or a qualified walking implementation.

The source contains eight authored closed tread/riser assemblies, separate nosings/materials, a lower landing review slab, and exact unchanged imported native roof r001 / floor r002 entrance panels. The blue object is a separate radius-0.3 m, total-height-1.8 m actor capsule review envelope. It is deliberately lifted to a conservative leading-radius support height; its apparent floating above a lower tread does **not** demonstrate a supported character gait. No GLB or runtime asset was exported.

`headroom.json` records exact source hashes, evaluated mesh bounds, and the clearance method. Every native panel surface is contained by its measured mesh AABB. Distance from the upright capsule axis to this containing AABB, minus the capsule radius, is a conservative lower bound on actual native surface clearance. The study tests the critical events of the piecewise constant leading-radius lift envelope and closest approach to the obstacle interval; between events the distance is minimized at the interval's clamped closest point. It covers the entrance center Y = 0.75..3 m. Beyond Y = 3 the retained panels end at Y = 2, giving at least 0.7 m horizontal capsule clearance regardless of Z. This same envelope contains either movement direction; it does not specify the movement controller or prove tread support during the lift.

The worst measured conservative capsule clearance is **0.349639323 m**, at center Y = 2.325 m and feet Z = 0.9375 m. This occurs diagonally around the slab corner after the capsule center passes beyond the roof edge. While the rectangular body footprint still overlaps the roof, the measured native underside is Z = 3 m and the highest conservative body top is Z = 2.55 m, preserving the earlier **0.45 m** vertical estimate. These two values measure different geometric cases.

The actual Cycles CPU render `roof-edge-capsule.png` was inspected. It shows the roof/floor slab edge, native stepped entrance and isolated blue envelope without intersections. The upper slab is cropped by the close camera; this evidence is scoped to its entrance edge. `capture.json` records the Blender version, camera, renderer and source hash. The complete editable source is `blender-source.blend`.

Remaining before ordinary stair walking can qualify: full dogleg geometry, actual rail and support intrusions, supported lift/forward/setdown envelopes for every riser in both directions, landing turns, safe underflight boundaries, higher ceiling clearance, complete native export and independent geometry preservation checks, actual actor/armor fit and authority/browser review. No pressure, structural load or artistic acceptance is implied. The current ladder source and runtime remain frozen.
