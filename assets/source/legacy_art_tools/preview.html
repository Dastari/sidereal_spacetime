SIDEREAL / CONSTRUCTION STUDY 01

One modular kit. Ships and places to walk.

Original Blender geometry exported to top-down tiles. This offline art preview tests scale and assembly; movement here is a mock-up, independent of the game server.

Explore the scale

Click the scene, then use WASD or arrow keys. Each tile is 2 metres and 64 pixels. Equipment blocks the preview character.

Separate layers

Floor, bulkheads, equipment, roof and characters share one grid. Toggle the roof to inspect the exterior footprint.

Production source

Editable .blend geometry, transparent albedo, local-space normal and emission atlases, individual PNGs and a metadata manifest accompany this page.

Connection masks: N=1, E=2, S=4, W=8. No game content has been published by this preview.

Angled exterior composition

Kestrel patrol frigate: a continuous tapered bow, central command spine, armoured shoulders and joined stern engine nacelles. This is an art composition, not yet a gameplay-validated hull blueprint. The offline 3D studies below use the Frontier Industrial palette.

3D presentation study

The same geometry assembled and lit in Blender, with an orthographic top-down camera and an angled inspection view. These are renders, not an in-engine performance benchmark.

3D ship interior study
