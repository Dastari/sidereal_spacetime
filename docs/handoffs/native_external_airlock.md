The ledger retains a001's failed outward-facing outer gasket, a002's corrected orientation, and the subsequent chamber/landing/route refinements. Regenerating the global library index is deferred until the current coordinated release freeze ends.

Seventy native placements form an 8 × 2 m overall supported footprint: a 2 × 2 m interior test room, 4 × 2 m airlock chamber, and 2 × 2 m unroofed exterior landing. The inner room/chamber use twelve quarter-floor cells and twelve roof surfaces, floor top 0.1875 m and roof underside 3 m. Four additional native floor cells support the exterior landing and contribute no enclosed gas volume. They have no railings and are not a docking/EVA connection.

The inner frame origin is `(2,0,0)`, quarter-turn 1. The outer frame is `(6,2,0)`, quarter-turn 3. Both hinges/gaskets face into the chamber. The deployed outer gasket needs that orientation to retain actual bottom support. Each door has independent frame, leaf, morphable gasket and seal-seat identities. The 4 m chamber permits a `(4,1,0.1875)` waiting position clear of both conservative 1.31 m hinge envelopes for the existing 0.3 m-radius body. Interior and exterior waiting positions are `(1,1,0.1875)` and `(7,1,0.1875)`.

The a007 audit independently hashes source dependencies, checks exact authored solids against exported triangles, computes new CSG cavities and native occupied/free volumes, measures bottom gasket coverage, evaluates the **actual exported SealRetracted morph** and accepted hinge rotations, and checks continuous full-height body sweeps. It passes **26 checks**, including inner-open/outer-closed travel from interior to chamber, outer-open/inner-closed travel from chamber to landing, and the both-open geometric diagnostic. The latter is geometry evidence, not authorization to open both doors. Total closed-reference free gas volume is 30.78371595325845 m³. Motion retains closed-reference volume; displaced-leaf pumping is not simulated. Coordinates use the existing 1 µm native audit tolerance and bounded 10 nm contact perturbation; no visual mesh is inflated to close gaps.

## Implemented pure contracts

`packages/sim/src/construction-airlock-plan.ts` verifies the exact new a007 audit and every native source byte before returning the exhaustive installation, independent instance-specific part/door/cell identities and its installation fingerprint. `acceptNativeExternalAirlockInstallation` compares actual server-owned placed identities, selected nodes, source hashes and transforms; a matching boolean is not accepted. Runtime authority must preserve this trust boundary: these inputs must never be exposed as client-supplied collider, pressure or installation parameters.

Its pressure compiler supplies two native-volume cells, sealed structural envelopes, a finite inner flow boundary and an outer vacuum boundary driven by **accepted** hinge/gasket state. Only deployed, fully closed qualified gaskets have zero flow. Existing conserved gas stepping accounts exterior venting; opening the outer leaf does not change gas in the closed inner room. Flow coefficients remain explicitly named gameplay policy, not material ratings.

`construction-airlock-controller.ts` composes the existing airlock decision and retract-before-hinge motion rules. Partly open hinges and partly retracted gaskets count as unclosed for opposing-door and pump interlocks. Every physical step rechecks current pressure, seal condition, power and obstruction. Power loss stops motion at its accepted pose and cancels pumping; it never snaps a leaf through a person. Passage requires a fully open accepted leaf, game permission, supported destination and permission to enter its environmental exposure. No emergency bypass or actor teleport is introduced.

A pump target currently records intent only. It **cannot add an unaudited reservoir/vent aperture, create gas, refill vacuum or silently consume electrical power**. A dedicated conserved resource/port adapter is required before pump controls become usable. Installation defaults to vacuum; tests explicitly allocate their gas inputs and are not a server spawn policy.

Twenty tests across the pure controller/plan/document and actual-GLB NullEngine loader cover exact native dependencies and installation mismatch, independent instance identities, accepted hinge/gasket sequencing, pressure and partial-door interlocks, supported passage, power/obstruction recovery, finite gas conservation, exterior vent accounting and no pump-created gas. `construction-airlock-document.ts` now provides an exact two-door semantic document, bounded parser, roof/landing role validation and exhaustive independent UUID remapping. The existing shared parser deliberately rejects its additive `airlockRoom` discriminator until matching authority and renderer registration land. Tests prove both independent remapped documents reload, altered floors/roof/doors/part mappings/old proofs reject, and the current shared parser cannot accidentally activate it.

`packages/render/src/native-airlock-scene.ts` now imports all 70 actual GLB placements with one load per used source (seven GLBs), preserves geometry/material sharing and independent placement IDs, binds two separate physical hinges and gasket morph managers, and consumes their independent accepted states. Missing/invalid states hide deployed gaskets. Native roofs alone respond to cutaway; all 16 floor modules, including the exterior landing, remain. Three NullEngine tests load the actual native bytes, verify roof/floor counts, independent leaf/gasket motion, hash/selector failures and full shared-resource cleanup. This is renderer plumbing, not a browser visual acceptance claim. During the release freeze it remains a separate loader; its common single-door import logic can be factored with `construction-pressure-room.ts` after that source boundary clears.
