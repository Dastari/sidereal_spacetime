# Native pressure room — bounded staged integration

2026-09-09, construction_resume. This is executable pressure compiler work and a native closure candidate, not a live airlock release. Planets, character/pose, inventory UI and the installed Wayfarer cockpit were not changed.

`packages/sim/src/construction-native-room.ts` loads the exact SHA-pinned native audit and all its GLB/contact-plan dependencies, then constructs one 4 × 2 m room from eight quarter tiles. A central 1.25 m doorway separates two finite-flow pressure compartments. It returns native installation placements, a required installation fingerprint, measured per-tile gas volumes and the existing pressure structure/topology. No caller-supplied layout or `validated: true` object is accepted. These are server/content factory inputs; do not expose them through reducer parameters.

The exact candidate is `assets/art-library/designs/shipyard.structure.boundary-kit/revisions/r006/final-a006/`. The qualified audit is `qualification-a007/native-room-validation.json`. GLB SHA is `415bd41cc9fc1e485be22c089a22c4e4961387b33d3402a1a40b063228a8fa69`; room audit SHA is `f347642d4ae424e63fa5abe0326f2e6cf0d82a5f168f0191c878f327056eec4e`. Editable Blender source, actual before/after/cutout CPU renders, reproducible derivation/audit scripts, part interfaces and a revision ledger fragment accompany it. The former design ledger was preserved byte-for-byte with SHA `eebf6e21759286c88a598afb68f45064949cdbcb3a3928789e7573b4580c4211` before changing the current revision pointer to 6. R005 is unchanged. Nothing is installed or owner art-approved by this record.

The new parts close native four-quarter floor junction holes through the floor body. Two interior plugs and one partition-context plug are required in this exact room. A third reusable mesh replaces the old r003 strip at the doorway's transverse floor seam, removing its intersection with r005 contact inserts. Do not install both strips. All Z coordinates already contain the 0.1875 m floor datum: only add deck elevation.

The complete exported room has a closed exterior in the native solid audit at 1 µm coordinate precision. The audit uses float64 CSG after quantizing native coordinates; a float32 CSG round trip created false microscopic contact slivers and is not the accepted result. R005 compound wedges use their exact authored patch decomposition, independently checked against the delivered GLB by the retained 130-check validation. New R006 meshes and other native solids are read directly from GLBs.

The physical closed interior is 20.166707416 m³ including shallow floor grooves. The fixed simulation volume above the walk datum is 20.166636779 m³ after actual wall/frame/leaf/material solid exclusion; the tiny below-datum grooves are explicitly excluded. The current model keeps these closed-reference cell volumes while the door moves; it does not simulate swept-volume pumping. This approximation is explicit and cannot be generalized to arbitrary geometry or damage.

The corrected a007 audit qualifies the exact static door seal. The former a006 audit is retained but superseded: merging touching native sub-solids before Boolean union incorrectly removed part of a door rail and changed free volume. The new audit evaluates original Blender sub-solids independently and requires their triangles to match the actual GLB exactly at 1 µm coordinate precision before union. Its small residual coplanar floor contact degeneracy is tested using a 10 nm perturbation, bounded below the declared tolerance, and independently requires full actual floor/threshold contact-area coverage. No visual mesh is inflated or replaced at runtime.

The compiler permits zero closed flow only with this exact native qualification and server-accepted hinge fraction zero AND gasket retraction zero. Any positive retraction restores finite closed-door flow. Opening requires fully retracted gasket state and retains finite flow without instant compartment merging. Policy coefficients apply to unseated/open flow and are named trusted gameplay tuning, not measured permeability or material ratings. Static contact qualification is not a completed powered/proximity-checked airlock or a general damage seal proof.

The native physical door plane is slightly offset from the semantic partition centre. Pressure cells use the declared tile partition discretization; measured tile-prism volumes are exact to the audit tolerance but this discretization assigns a small door alcove to the nominal tile's compartment. It is a bounded simulation approximation, not a claim of exact continuous cavity partition volumes.
