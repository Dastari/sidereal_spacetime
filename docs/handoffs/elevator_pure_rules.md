# Elevator mechanics prerequisite

2026-09-10. Source-only pure rules in `packages/sim/src/construction-elevator.ts`, with32 meaningful mechanics tests in `construction-elevator.test.ts`. This is **not an installed lift, approved geometry, functional rating or powered game feature**. No content catalog, package export, world schema/reducer, utility allocator, generated binding, renderer, App or service was changed. Existing stairs/ladders remain separate controllers.

## Implemented slice

- Definition/state validation keeps instance, installed platform, cabin door, all landing doors, power port and exact proof/revision references explicit. Stops have actual deck IDs and ordered metre datums. Coordinates are ship-local metres, Z up. The rectangular cabin envelope includes the shell; its uninterrupted interior floor is a separate support envelope.
- Persistent passenger/cargo attachments retain existing entity IDs, source deck/location revision, load revision, mass and platform-local body bounds. Boarding requires an aligned held cabin, actual fully open doors, authority and an external handling reservation. Overlap, missing floor contact, overhead clipping, overload, duplicate identity and unrestrained cargo reject. No inventory is created or copied. This slice supports multiple independently placed bodies on a continuous cabin floor, **not cargo stacks or arbitrary union-bearing patches**; the existing cargo stack validator must qualify those separately.
- Calls have a bounded FIFO queue and separate command revision, so automatic motion updates do not invalidate every operator command. Permissions/reach and destination IDs are server-derived inputs. Queued cancellation requires the caller's own request and expected command revision; an active destination cannot be erased while moving. Durable operation receipts—including retries after a call has completed—remain a required world responsibility.
- Accepted car and landing door fractions/latched states gate motion. A target animation value does not count as a closed door. Missing, duplicate or malformed accepted door snapshots cannot enable motion/boarding. A stopped car must be exactly aligned and held by a rated passive brake before a corresponding landing-open request is emitted. Remote landing doors do not open through this controller. Door requests themselves do not change collision or claim door energy expenditure; an actual powered door controller must accept and account them.
- Drive motion uses fixed50 ms steps with piecewise acceleration, speed limiting, braking and an exact stop event inside the step. A large elapsed tick cannot cause catch-up. The car never snaps across distance to reset to a deck. Before powered movement, the full swept cabin and passive stopping envelope are checked against accepted shaft obstacles and stop limits, rather than checking just the next endpoint.
- A drive allocation identifies the elevator, connected power port, tick, prior state revision and available joules. The returned debit must commit atomically with accepted motion in the real utility ledger. Short, wrong-port, stale-tick or wrong-revision allocations cannot fund movement. Work-per-loaded-kilogram/metre and running losses are explicit input definitions; no Earth gravity, perpetual power or regeneration is assumed.
- Power or destination-permission loss applies bounded passive deceleration when a qualified brake can hold the actual loaded mass. Cabin and attachments remain together. Restored accounted power can resume from that supported braked state. No brake or an invalidated stopping envelope produces `recovery-required`, preserving the last accepted record and requiring an explicit qualified recovery/support-loss adapter. **This is not a simulation of falling or an assertion that an unbraked moving car magically stays supported.** Such installations cannot be activated through this slice.
- Disembarkation requires actual dock/doors and a separate reserved, qualified destination body/support supplied by the native landing adapter. Its result requests an atomic entity/deck/location update using the existing location/load revisions. Simply moving through a deck height never changes an occupant's source deck.
- Duplicate ticks produce no second energy debit. A stationary unused elevator returns its exact original state rather than manufacturing idle clock writes. Disconnect is intentionally absent from attachment deletion logic: authority may remove input/call access, but it must retain passengers, cargo and loaded mass.

## Authority and geometry adapters required before registration

1. **Native asset qualification.** Author the actual cabin, shaft, platform, guides, drive/brake, landing doors/sills, access apertures and overhead clearances in Blender. Preserve exact source hashes, measured solids and support. The pure validator checks consistency of supplied rectangles/ratings; a hexadecimal `proofHash` is not proof of native geometry. Only a trusted native installation compiler may construct production definitions from approved source and gameplay metadata. Synthetic test values are never asset stats.
2. **Functional metadata.** Supply explicitly accepted drive acceleration/speed, empty/load mass, capacity, passive brake holding/deceleration and energy/loss policy. Do not infer them from the model or enable travel with a `powered` flag. Keep structural shaft/platform damage separate from drive/controller entity health. No airtightness or pressure rating is supplied by this code.
3. **Utility allocation/debit.** Resolve connected real supply/reservoir rows and reserve energy for the exact car step, doors and controls. Persist their debit and motion atomically. Enforce uniqueness/replay in the energy ledger. The pure function proposes a debit and does not itself subtract from a battery. There is no existing generic world elevator power allocator to wire blindly.
4. **Private state and current authority.** Register separate car state, attachment, queue/receipt and utility reservation rows. Check game admission and lease at command consumption, actual landing reach or attached-passenger access, expected command/location/load revisions, current destination access, load changes and attachment reservations. Serialize concurrent boarding, cargo edits, door commands and dispatch. Recheck permission at arrival and disembarkation; do not let one passenger's permissions grant another general workspace access.
5. **Accepted door/shaft collision.** Gather actual door rows and all native shell, shaft, landing, threshold and dynamic actor/cargo swept obstacles. No client boolean certifies clear space. Door sweeps/actuation/pressure seals remain separate measured mechanisms. A moving obstruction invalidating a previously accepted brake corridor must go through an explicit recovery/support-loss policy, not a teleport.
6. **Egress and recovery.** Design an explicit passenger-safe destination change/maintenance rescue route. Current active-call cancellation deliberately rejects; restoring the same destination's access can resume, but arbitrary emergency rerouting is not implemented. A workspace-denied actor may retain only its own attachment/status and minimum immutable egress geometry, following the stair pattern. Never restore the private ship document as a convenience. Recovery energy must come from an accounted installed battery/drive or qualified manual mechanism; no free emergency trip exists here.
7. **World commit and lifecycle.** Preserve character, container/item, ship/deck, attachment and platform UUIDs. Update attached accepted XYZ atomically with the cabin and commit a new deck only after validated disembarkation. Persist queue/energy checkpoints across restart without elapsed-time catch-up. Refit/removal/damage cannot erase occupied support/reservations. Last accepted unsupported records are not final destruction physics; a defined fall/restraint/rescue adapter is required before damage readiness.
8. **Client/editor integration.** Later renderer consumes authoritative cabin/attachment XYZ and door poses, never simulates its own lift trip. Shipyard requires shaft openings across actual floor/roof panels, distinct stops, maintenance clearance, utility connectivity and native placement qualification. No demonstration cabin has been inserted into Wayfarer.

## Validation and next gate

The focused test suite covers multiple passengers/cargo, load/headroom/support/restraint rejection, actual cabin/remote/missing/unlatched door interlocks, stopped alignment, permission/reach/revision checks, independent command revision, FIFO/cancellation, exact up travel, bounded power-loss braking, no catch-up, restored-power recovery, absent brake, invalid allocations, shaft stopping-envelope collision, explicit disembark and duplicate ticks/idle states. Focused TypeScript passes.

No native/game/browser elevator evidence or isolated SpacetimeDB elevator smoke exists because no elevator is registered. The next appropriate step is trusted geometry/functional/utility interface design, followed by private authority and real powered multi-passenger tests. Keep this as a prerequisite alongside the accepted construction roadmap, not a completed multi-deck ship or airlock feature.
