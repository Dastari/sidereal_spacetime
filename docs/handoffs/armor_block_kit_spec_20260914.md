# Reusable backed hull kit r005

Status: implementation specification for owner-requested next native review. Exact artistic and live publication approvals remain separate.

## Objective

Replace thin decorated side sheets and configuration-specific bow pods with a common, substantial exterior armor kit. The accepted Wayfarer concept remains the visual target. A player should choose a boundary run, armor depth/height and finish; the construction system should select the compatible native span and junction recipes, retain their individual identities, and preview the complete result.

## Contract

The new review family declares a **1.0 m total outward reservation**, measured from the unchanged outer structural edge: **0.75 m armor backing + 0.25 m exterior frame/finish**. This supersedes the r004 visual depth only for this new family; it does not widen any existing qualification, collision or live pin. Interior structural thickness remains 250 mm inward. Heights are 0.75, 1.5, 2.25 and 3 m. Backing must occupy the actual intermediate volume and be independently visible when decorative finish is hidden; offsetting a thin shell fails.

Source coordinates use Blender X/Y in the floorplan and Z up. Floor endpoints remain integer 1/32 m coordinates; orientations derive from the exact directed endpoint vector, with no independently rounded yaw. Admit the current 32 floor directions (axis, 1:1, 1:2, 1:4, 1:8 and quarter-turn/reflected equivalents). Exact diagonal lengths and corner profiles are authored in Blender; runtime does not stretch a 2 m cuboid. A compact user-facing kit may contain numerous exact native adapters selected automatically.

Span and corner asset IDs describe geometry parameters (length/endpoint vector, height, corner rays, convexity and finish), never Wayfarer placement IDs or cockpit-specific names. Joint blocks own corner material without positive-volume overlap; span ends meet named contact planes. Concave corners and acute turns need measured cutbacks, fit checks and explicit rejection when adjacent runs are too short. Sharp exterior spikes must be bounded by a deliberate beveled outer joint, not hidden beyond its reservation. Vertical courses use unchanged fixed-scale features and matching lower/upper contacts.

Use actual backing, stepped upper/lower shoulders and generous pale wrapped connectors for silhouette and bulk. Recessed plain/red/vent/utility/identity faces share portable color, normal and roughness maps. Fine seams, marks, fasteners and shallow relief belong in maps. Deep louvers and silhouette changes remain geometry. Separate BACKING and FINISH exports; sources keep editable named solids/materials/sockets. No specialized engine cutouts or hull filler pods. Replace the previous special front shoulder/cheek/bumper art in the review fixture with generic straight, angled, convex/concave and height-transition pieces. Cockpit glazing, inner rooms, engines and thrusters remain unchanged unless the common envelope reveals a genuine documented interference.

## Decision record: keyed boundary kit instead of free-angle placement

Accepted for this requested native pass. A freely rotated standard-width panel has the correct orientation but generally the wrong endpoint distance, and rectangular strips overlap at concave corners. A purely Wayfarer-specific front mesh cannot support player construction. Use endpoint-vector keyed native spans, reusable corner/transition blocks and a boundary assembly plan that selects them. This permits a small visible construction vocabulary while preserving exact physical interfaces. The cost is a larger offline native adapter catalog and explicit unsupported-fit reporting. Native authoring may share recipe functions and maps; geometric catalog variants remain separate from live placed-object identities. The 1.0 m new reservation requires future authority/collision qualification rather than silently inheriting r004 approval.

### Assembly interface refinement

The Astra reviewer confirms all 32 directions but distinguishes them from corner-pair coverage. This pass qualifies the finite turn combinations used by the proofs and lists other pairs explicitly unsupported. Corner end profiles determine exact remaining span lengths; negative spans fail, and zero remaining spans are allowed only where adjacent qualified corner ports meet exactly. Do not overlap full-length floor-edge strips with corner fillers.

The unchanged floor union for Wayfarer is CCW, metres: [(-5,-9),(5,-9),(5,9),(3,9),(3,11),(1,13),(-1,13),(-3,11),(-3,9),(-5,9)]. Mount at floor top Z=0.1875. Side/aft armor is 3 m; common cockpit runs are 1.5 m, with reusable height terminations. The old Pilot corner-buttress, outer shoulder transition, special sill/cheek/bumper and roof collar visuals are removed only from private review rendering, while original document and qualification remain intact.

The thicker external ring intersects original equipment mount positions. For review, retain exact engine/thruster assets and orientation, and move each outward rigidly to the new exterior face using measured native bounds; record old/new mount planes and deltas. No configuration-specific armor cutouts. These review mount transformations do not alter live entities or constitute qualified gameplay mounts. Future installation needs explicit common mount adapters and authority/collision checks against the declared new reservation.
