1. Pin the semantic document, compiler version, all native asset hashes, qualified support/collision evidence, low-step limits and generated projection schema. Reject changed or unqualified geometry. The server selects support and commits elevation; the client only renders accepted values. Low steps are not arbitrary stair or jump capability.
2. Connect newly allocated instance/deck/placed-object identities to private inventory containers and existing approved fitting behavior. Empty containers are the default. Any initial loadout creates fresh item UUIDs under the same authoritative spawn transaction; it never clones a player's possessions. 3. Scope all views/actions to the actual accepted construction visit, instance, deck, reach, clear line of sight and grants. A nearby rendered object does not create access. Original-ship storage must not remain actionable while the actor is in a different construction frame. Return restores the original actor context without discarding its carried items or appearance.
4. Bind ordinary click/selection/proximity interactions to the spawned object IDs. Seat occupancy and light toggles persist per instance. Preserve ownership/revision/operation validation and clear control appropriately on disconnect/leave. A decorative reactor or engine mesh is not automatically a powered, rated component; unimplemented functions remain unavailable.

## Following gates toward the complete ship

- Author intentional additional decks and supported stairs; elevators require actual platform/passenger/cargo attachments, power/interlocks and failure/recovery. A ladder/stair fixture does not qualify the entire ship as a sealed multi-deck vessel.
- Bind structural walls/roofs and real door/airlock assemblies to enclosure proofs. Charge atmosphere from a defined conserved resource; test pressure split/merge, breach, door obstruction and external egress/reentry. Current native pressure fixtures start in vacuum.
- Standardize cargo bearing surfaces/heights and prove mixed-size support union, overload, removing support, door/lid clearance and restraint. Visual AABBs and color variants are not stacking or payload ratings.
- Connect 3D power/fuel/data/coolant/ventilation routes to typed ports and actual component supply. Then bind an authored instance to a real authoritative shared-world ship and derive supported flight/control capabilities from validated installed definitions; do not inherit stock fixture thrust just because an engine mesh is present.
- Preserve the owner damage split: localized voxel damage for structural/hull/armor and explicitly classified exterior components; entity health and later authored damage states for internal equipment/containers. Prove visible native breaches agree with collision, gas/support/services, repair and independent instance state.
- Only after the full template is qualified, perform a separate explicit no-loss existing-Wayfarer migration. Preserve current inventory/crew/fitting UUIDs and resources atomically. Spawn-new and refit-existing are distinct operations with different identity rules.
