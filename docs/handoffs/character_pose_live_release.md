## Integration behavior

The world supplies accepted aim, item UUID and shot sequence plus movement, facing, visibility, seating, sprint and reduced-motion state. The controller runs after animation evaluation; the beam reads its final physical muzzle afterward and clips along that actual direction. Cursor aim never independently steers the beam. Active movement retains independent travel/aim, including strafe/backward remapping in the current solved torso frame. Lowered and sprint movement follows travel. Hidden/seated states suppress the muzzle effect. Sprint/lowered/unequip states cannot re-enter the raised-pose fallback after release; inactive diagnostics are cleared each frame. Binding generations discard late loads; unbinding precedes equipment disposal. Clearance padding comes from the bounds of the actually equipped chest, shoulder and helmet components, with the cosmetic armor category used only for legacy comparison rigs. Per-item history seeds without replaying old shots.

The paper doll uses the same published equipment/controller and supports rotation on its holographic disc. It has no combat-event authority and does not synthesize accepted shots. Unsupported visual items retain the canonical attachment fallback.

No simulation coordinates, permissions, inventory rules, network schema, hit/damage authority or production database publication are changed. Only the managed client was refreshed during integration; other managed services are preserved.
