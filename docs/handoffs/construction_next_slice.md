# Next construction slice: one authoritative two-deck ladder connection

2026-09-09. Proposed next implementation, based on the current tree and [owner construction contract](../ship_construction_rebuild.md). This document changes no runtime behavior, publishes no assets and does not mark the multi-deck or Wayfarer milestone complete. Planets remain paused. Root coordinates shared integration; the external agent retains character models/poses and inventory UI ownership.

Deliver one separately published, native two-deck review template with a real ladder shaft, a safe landing on each deck, persistent server-controlled traversal and two independent instance spawns. Use a manual ladder first: it establishes deck/shaft identity and controlled vertical movement without requiring a powered lift or arbitrary sloped walking. Keep the existing R006 pressure room as its own bounded fixture. Adding a second deck or cutting its roof invalidates its exact qualification.

## Current implementation and remaining gaps

- `ConstructionDocument` and layout intent already support eight decks. Published documents, server UUID remapping, private grants/receipts, independent instances and topology-driven walking exist. `constructionDeck` holds actual deck IDs, elevation and ceiling; `constructionLocation.deckId` is already the effective occupied deck during construction review. Earlier handoffs describing all of this as absent are historical.
- `construction-instance.ts` explicitly leaves `mappings.traversalLinks` and `cargoGrids` empty. The document has no traversal declaration, physical shaft aperture, link reservation, movement phase or destination admission contract. A hole seed only identifies a void already formed by floor topology; placing a seed does not cut a solid floor or roof.
- `stepActor` selects one deck and sweeps a planar circle. No reducer authorizes a physical deck transition. `loadConstructionInstance` loads the requested deck, and renderer composition places the existing character at a deck walking elevation. Selecting an editor deck cannot become movement authority.
- Native floor/roof kits provide fixed module/datums and validated overlap checks. Roof generation currently mirrors floor placements. A ladder needs an explicit, matching ceiling/floor opening and protected landing; no installed ladder/stair/lift interface was found in the content contracts or assembly catalog. A new preserved Blender asset/interface is required before the browser can demonstrate physical traversal.
- The R006/a007 room now has private persistent pressure and accepted gasket/hinge state, a bounded fixed clock, exact installation validation and vacuum initialization. Its qualified scope is one 4 × 2 m, one-deck, no-equipment room. It is neither a general enclosure compiler nor a powered airlock. Current browser/isolated smoke outcomes belong in their actual evidence records, not in this proposal.

## Native fixture and dimensional contract

Use an isolated lower work platform and upper service landing, not an invented extra Wayfarer deck. A practical initial layout is two 6 × 6 m structural footprints on the existing 2 m grid, with a central 2 × 2 m upper-floor shaft void and a corresponding lower-ceiling aperture. The lower deck retains the floor below the shaft. A preserved native ladder/guard/landing assembly connects the two. These fixture dimensions are a proposed authoring request; no mesh or rating is certified by this document.

| Constraint | Existing exact value or required rule |
| --- | --- |
| Authoring lattice / module | 32 units/m; 64-unit (2 m) structural module. Quarter modules are explicit compatible parts. |
| Lower deck origin / walking datum | Origin 0; floor top 6 units = 0.1875 m. |
| Lower roof | Underside 96 units = 3 m; backing top 100 units; full native visual envelope top 102 units = 3.1875 m. |
| Proposed upper origin | 102 units is the existing minimum before any explicit service allowance. Upper walking datum is 108 units = 3.375 m; walking-plane separation is 3.1875 m. Do not use a 96-unit origin that intersects the lower roof. |
| Standing clearance | With the existing 96-unit ceiling and 6-unit floor top: 90 units = 2.8125 m. Existing authority review body is radius 0.3 m and height 1.8 m; these are collision-policy dimensions, not a claim that every armor mesh fits. |
| Aperture and landing | Native free aperture, handrail intrusion, mount/dismount corridor and destination capsule must be measured after all actual solids. A nominal 2 m void is not automatically 2 m usable clearance. |
| Roof exclusion | Declare the shaft aperture in construction content and match it to actual native panel omission/edge parts. A cutaway, invisible roof or hole seed cannot authorize passage through an intact solid. |
| Native source frame | Blender XY is ship planar space; Blender Z is height. Rendering maps planar Y to −Z. Existing part Z includes its authored floor datum where declared; do not add the datum twice. |
| Materials and structure | Preserve Blender surfaces, source files and exact node/source hashes. Collision/shaft traversal volumes are explicit separate representations. No mass, load, seal or armor rating follows from GLB bounds. |

The roof adapter must distinguish a real shaft opening from the normal roof generated over a floor. For this first fixture, constrain it to the one approved grid-aligned aperture and exact native adapter. Guard the upper opening so ordinary planar walking cannot fall or clip through it; the only opening through that guard is the validated ladder mount/dismount corridor. General free falling, jumping and six-degree-of-freedom movement remain separate work.

A nominal overlay or untextured debug box can help unit tests, but the playable acceptance fixture needs the authored ladder, guards and matching roof/floor opening. Start its art work at the living library, preserve each meaningful revision and request exact artistic sign-off separately from the already authorized implementation work.

## Smallest authority contract

Add a versioned optional construction traversal section with one supported adapter initially. It declares a template-local link ID; source and destination deck IDs; the exact native ladder/shaft revision; source/destination standing anchors; a directed authored movement path; protected landing/corridor volumes; and the real roof/floor aperture references. These are validated publication inputs. Client requests provide the installed link ID and intent, never a target transform, height, duration or destination deck.

Extend server UUID allocation to traversal links and aperture objects. Remap all references through the existing instance mapping; reject duplicate/missing/cross-instance references before any rows are inserted. Preserve old documents with an absent traversal section as having no links. Do not reinterpret an old room name, fitting label or empty `traversalLinks` array as a new connection.

Use additive private tables:

- `constructionTraversalLink`: installed link ID, instance/source-link mapping, two actual deck IDs, pinned adapter/geometry hash, accepted instance revision, canonical path/landing contract and link revision.
- `constructionTraversal`: one active row per character, stable traversal UUID, link/instance/visit IDs, source and destination deck IDs, accepted source/destination anchors, fixed-step progress, direction/phase, accepted instance/link revisions and interruption reason. Store this state; never reconstruct an in-progress journey from reconnect time.
- `constructionTraversalReservation`: exclusive link reservation plus protected source/destination landing footprints and holder identity. One ladder user at a time is the first declared capacity policy, not an inferred structural load rating. Retain a bounded completion/cancellation receipt/audit after releasing the reservation.

For construction review, retain `constructionLocation.deckId` as the one effective occupied-deck authority. Do not add a competing character-deck row for the same review frame. During transit it remains the source deck, while the traversal row defines the accepted path position and interaction restriction; change the deck and planar anchor atomically on successful arrival. The later live-ship migration can introduce a general actor-location companion through an explicit conversion, rather than silently moving all legacy characters now.

A begin request validates game admission, connected actor, owned/access-permitted instance under the current scope, matching visit and occupied source deck, workspace capability, exact instance/link revisions, source-side reach/clear path, standing state, usable native adapter and free reservation/landing. Use the existing expected-revision/operation-receipt mechanism; permission checks precede replay acceptance. Broad visitor/crew boarding permission is not added merely to test the ladder.

The accepted mounting path begins at the actor’s actual server position and reaches the authored entry anchor through the validated approach corridor; proximity alone must not snap the actor to that anchor. Once accepted, clear movement, flight and aim inputs and reserve both landings. The server advances a bounded path parameter on fixed ticks; elapsed wall time does not create catch-up. A named manual review traversal duration is gameplay tuning, not a native actuator rating. Recheck actor/instance/link validity and landing protection during advancement and at completion. Ordinary movement commands cannot move the actor concurrently, and item drop/pickup, seating and other reach actions must not treat the actor as standing on either landing while in transit. Coordinate these narrow authority guards with the external inventory owner; no inventory UI redesign is part of this slice.

On a clear arrival, commit destination deck, destination anchor, traversal completion and reservation release together, then clear stale inputs again. On cancellation, grant loss, disconnect or an actor becoming unable to act, preserve the accepted in-transit position and reverse along the same validated path toward the reserved source landing at the fixed rate. Keep the source landing reserved until the actor returns. Do not instantly teleport, fill gas, grant control or restart the original operation on reconnect. If physical invalidation makes even the reserved return path unsafe, persist a blocked traversal and require a separately validated recovery action; the fixed first fixture rejects refit/removal while occupied or reserved, so ordinary editing cannot create that state.

Process restart must resume this persisted state without a second grant/resource allocation. Review exit must either reject while traversal is active or begin the normal validated return-to-source path; the existing review return transform must not bypass a live ladder reservation. Identity migration must explicitly include the new ownership/reference rows or reject while a traversal is active, preserving the character and item UUIDs.

## Required acceptance tests

1. Publication rejects mismatched source hashes, absent actual apertures, bad roof/upper-floor separation, obstructed native landing/corridor, unsupported climb dimensions, cyclic or cross-instance links and incomplete ID mappings. Old documents remain supported without acquiring traversal capability.
2. Begin rejects wrong principal, missing/expired/revoked capability, foreign visit, wrong source deck, insufficient reach, seated/incompatible action, stale instance/link/location revision and occupied link. Exact retry returns the original traversal identity without creating another reservation. The reservation rule is tested against two prospective authorized actors without expanding live access policy as an incidental effect.
3. Progress is finite and bounded; movement cannot leave the authored swept corridor. Ordinary deck walking cannot cross the guarded hole, move a reserved landing occupant or overwrite transit state. Arrival changes deck once and only at the accepted destination. Camera or draft deck selection cannot change it.
4. Cancellation, disconnect/reconnect, permission loss, input replay and actual process restart retain/reverse the same traversal. No duplicate completion, residual reservation, catch-up jump, stale thrust/aim command or repeated allocation remains. A blocked landing never causes teleport/clamping to an arbitrary nearby point.
5. Spawn two instances from one blueprint. Every deck/link/aperture/fitting UUID is distinct between instances; template-local mapping is preserved. Traversing, canceling, changing a permitted door or reconnecting in one does not alter the other.
6. Record character, ship, every carried item/container/fitting and review return UUID/contents before and after. Transit creates no inventory and changes no contents, balances or equipment IDs. New templates remain empty unless an independently approved and accounted loadout explicitly supplies new resources.
7. Real browser review walks to the ladder, traverses both directions and walks on both actual native decks. Capture the real opening/guards, accepted progress, deck elevation, cutaway/roof behavior, denial cases, sibling-instance isolation and ordinary reload. Temporary debug actor transforms or a preview scene do not meet this gate.
8. Run `npm run check`, `npm run build`, applicable asset validation and isolated authority smoke with restart coverage. Use managed lifecycle commands and additive publication; retain actual outputs and distinguish unit tests, browser evidence and measured performance.

Passing this establishes one real controlled multi-deck connection. It does not complete ship entry/EVA, cargo stacking, powered services, pressure certification, localized structural damage or a fully reconstructed Wayfarer.

## Pressure, utilities and subsequent Wayfarer route

The traversal fixture starts without pressure qualification and without a gas charge. Do not clone R006 closed-room gas/certificates into both decks or treat a roof cutaway as a gas opening. Its open ladder shaft will require a separately measured free-volume and cross-deck flow adapter before pressurization is enabled. When that adapter exists, the shaft connects accepted volumes through a finite flow edge; topology changes conserve existing gas and account for any removed volume. It cannot silently initialize both decks to cabin pressure.

The current pressure room's exact sealed static pose, gameplay flow tuning and fixed closed-reference free volume remain bounded. Nonzero atmosphere needs a named resource debit/allocation; supply/pump/vent transfers, conserved resources and utility power policy are still required. `transitionAirlock` currently supplies pure policy only. A real external airlock additionally needs two installed doors, chamber volume, pumps/vents, actuator power, pressure-differential checks, obstruction and emergency outcomes plus an actual authorized exterior/reentry frame.

After the ladder slice, use the current cockpit and complete 262-placement Wayfarer audits to classify every existing native placement and stable live association. Design only useful additional Wayfarer deck space; do not copy the review platforms into the ship as a pretend conversion. Build the semantic floor/boundary/roof/shaft topology with the same compiler and native interfaces, then add cargo-only grids/support unions, 3D utility routes, validated external mounts and explicit damage representations. Each missing interface becomes a narrow native or authority task, rather than an invented gameplay rating.

Save this as a new immutable authored Wayfarer blueprint and spawn two independent empty/loadout-accounted instances through the normal path. Existing live Wayfarer conversion is a distinct explicit migration: verify source and expected live revisions, preserve the existing ship/character/item/fitting/container UUIDs and contents, map occupied stations and decks, carry gas/fuel/energy/damage state with explicit accounting, revalidate control/access and write audit/tombstones atomically. A blueprint spawn, capture operation or account reconnect must never perform that migration automatically.

## Conservative doorway sweep: planned narrow refinement

The accepted door controller currently uses the entire native r001 −90° hinge sweep AABB for every attempted hinge or gasket step. In the door's authored frame it spans X `[0.3125, 1.6244895197752154]` m and Y `[-1.3744895197752154, 0]` m; the reported Z span is approximately `[0.1895, 2.4355]` m. `doorSweepOccupied` applies the complete instance quarter-turn/origin and tests a radius-0.3 m actor circle against that planar box. In the pressure room the complete doorway frame is `[2, 0]`, quarter-turn 1.

This conservatively reserves empty corners of the box and the full remaining swing even when the next accepted 50 ms step moves only a small angle. It can block opening, closing or gasket deployment when an actor is clear of the actual next geometry. Current collision keeps the rotated leaf after full opening; passage only becomes available at hinge fraction 1. The native seal controller still requires gasket retraction before any hinge opening and both fractions exactly zero for qualified zero flow. None of that accepted behavior changes in this document.

A later narrow adapter should retain the existing AABB as broad phase, then use a conservative continuous sweep of the actual pinned leaf convex pieces over the *proposed accepted angle interval*, expanded by the authority actor capsule. Use an analytic rotational bound or bounded subdivision with a proved arc-error expansion; endpoint-only angle samples can miss a hit. Gasket extension/retraction needs its own swept envelope from the pinned native morph/contact geometry, rather than borrowing the hinge envelope. Recheck physical vertical overlap by authoritative deck/traversal location so a character on a different deck cannot block this door merely by sharing XY.

Test hinge-near and latch-near contacts, interior AABB corners that are actually clear, actors entering between ticks, partial-angle reversal, exact closed/open endpoints, all four quarter turns, translated instances and both gasket directions. The narrow phase must never accept a motion that collides with the authored body/leaf sweep; uncertain or over-budget cases retain conservative blocking. Only accepted pose changes feed pressure/collision. The existing static seal proof must not be weakened to improve interaction convenience.
