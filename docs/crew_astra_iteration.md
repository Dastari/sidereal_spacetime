## Preserved structure

The original `assets/source/crew-frontier.blend` is preserved. New editable work is `assets/source/crew-astra.blend`, generated by `scripts/build_crew_source.py` with original modular geometry in `scripts/build_crew_archetypes.py`. The curved visors are closed authored optical shells: alpha .82, transmission .08, IOR 1.46, roughness .18 and clearcoat .5. They are explicitly excluded from opaque helmet occupancy sampling. Export asserts optical material retention. Runtime recolors only material roles; selective light color/intensity are assigned once, preserving authored strength 1.3 despite glTF export normalization.

## Image iterations

1. All ten source PNGs exported and compared directly against exact reference crops. This revealed pastel studio lighting, small heads, hidden pilot stripes and undersized medic markings. First images are preserved locally in `.runtime/crew-iteration-one`.
2. Reduced key/fill exposure, stronger contrast, head surface scale 1.22/1.18/1.16 around the unchanged pivot, a larger medical cross and pilot stripes following crown courses. Comparison showed improved proportion and color but persistent flat pressure-shell corners.
3. Octagonal molded pressure-shell courses, dark recon lens rings with smaller emissive cores, industrial mechanic ear modules, stronger medic crown/side bands and officer cap bands. These remain original editable geometry, not images pasted onto the model.

Canonical source renders are `assets/runtime/crew/looks/<look>.png`; `portrait.png` is the engineer in a neutral pose for the inventory paper doll. `looks/manifest.json` records exact palettes and visible source solids. The GLB manifest records source/runtime hashes, bounds, weighted batches, optics and animations. Regenerate through `npm run art:crew`; it does not copy public application assets.

4. Owner rear-view screenshot exposed missing helmet backs. Added rear shell, corner coverage, neck seal and service vents to every enclosed helmet, plus hair/back-band coverage for open headgear. Every look now exports front/side/rear PNGs under `looks/views/` as well as the three-quarter reference view.
