# Native hull finish study

Status: isolated r001 material candidate reviewed in a valid matched closed-hull game comparison. No publication recommended; the full plastic target is not met. Nothing published or finally approved.

The installed pilot hull comes from the external hull author's native Blender r001 kit, with 34 placed modules. The owner authorized its interim publication while requesting a more tapered/raked r002. That active geometry revision remains owned by the hull author. This study does not claim or advance it.

The earlier `scripts/ship_materials.py` experiment was staged against the legacy voxel ship and was never accepted or published. It cannot affect the newer `loadInstalledHull` assets. The installed clean hull paint exports metallic .35 and a constant roughness texture of 130/255 (about .51); graphite seals are metallic .4/roughness .37, and red access parts metallic .2/roughness .5. There is no clearcoat. The loader retains those GLB material properties. Existing game lighting uses exterior specular .3 and HDR environment intensity .28. These facts explain why an earlier code experiment did not produce the requested in-game plastic finish.

The scoped draft is [native-r001-polymer-01](../assets/art-library/shipyard-hull/material-studies/native-r001-polymer-01/study.json). It starts from the immutable editable r001 Blender source, preserves every mesh/modifier, material color, normal/base texture, placement and proxy, and changes only selected material inputs. Paint is dielectric with roughness .30, coat .12, coat roughness .20 and IOR 1.46. Graphite seals become nonmetallic/matte (.62); red accents use dielectric roughness .28 and coat .08. Titanium hardware, glazing and emitter records remain unchanged. Original roughness textures remain packed in the source; the clean constant roughness link is replaced by an explicit scalar. No worn/damage-state or gameplay change is implied.

All ten staged GLB exports have exactly equal geometry-accessor hashes and primitive counts to their published equivalents. Glass/titanium/emitter material records compare equal. Native source geometry and modifier digests also compare equal. The clearcoat lobe adds shader work but no lights, draw primitives, textures or triangles; target-hardware timing remains unmeasured.

Actual matched Blender panel PNGs are saved beside the source and inspected. The candidate is a cleaner/brighter dielectric but its difference is modest. The source still has single-segment chamfers (18 mm on this hull panel), a broad flat field and limited reference hardware. A material-only study cannot substitute for the external author's planned silhouette and surface redesign. Runtime before/after used unchanged game lighting and response-only GLB overrides, with no canonical asset copy.

## Actual runtime result

The valid [closed-hull comparison](../assets/art-library/shipyard-hull/material-studies/native-r001-polymer-01/comparison.png) uses the same 1120 × 800 viewport, camera and production sun/HDR lighting. Loaded candidate PBR values confirm metallic 0, roughness .30, IOR 1.46 and enabled .12 clearcoat. Geometry/camera equality and GL errors 0 were recorded separately from visual inspection. A fixed roof crop changed mean RGB from about (130,140,160) to (138,147,168): the finish is somewhat brighter, but broad panels still look flat and lack the reference's rounded manufactured edge response. No large new white highlight patches appear in the valid closed pair. This is not sufficient plastic-look acceptance and does not justify publishing the candidate.

The baseline interior image also exposes existing jagged white floor patches and trim artifacts. The candidate's first interior frame failed its image gate: the environment and marker rendered while the ship's shaders were still warming. That image is retained as invalid evidence in `capture.json`; material-value checks and GL error 0 did not prove the frame was visually ready. Repeated whole-scene readiness polling additionally hit a Babylon reused-render-target UBO error in the diagnostic; warmed closed-view renders succeeded without that query. A correctly warmed interior pair is still needed if this candidate proceeds. GPU was handed onward after the bounded capture batch.

The concrete next step is coordination with the structural author: evaluate the dielectric finish alongside r002's native panel/corner shapes and a rounded bevel profile, while separately fixing existing floor/trim artifacts. The material-only r001 trial is preserved, not marked as owner-approved or as a finished ship redesign. Existing equipment is untouched.

## R006 material-only continuation

A separate [native-r006-polymer-01 study](../assets/art-library/shipyard-hull/material-studies/native-r006-polymer-01/review.md) now uses the preserved R006 Blender source and the combined installed R006 hull/r002 floor. The floor's baked ORM and geometry are retained. Seventeen staged native exports and the editable derivative preserve all geometry, textures and non-target material records; fifteen exports are actually placed and were loaded by hash-matched browser overrides.

The valid matched game bow pair demonstrates slightly brighter edges, still below the requested premium-plastic finish. No derivative has been published or accepted. The earlier assumption that exterior `Light.specular=.3` suppresses the native directional PBR highlight was incorrect: installed Babylon's PBR shader uses the light's diffuse radiance for that lobe. The pixel-identical `.65` light-specular control is not a useful PBR tuning result. A material-local specular/environment-response comparison is prepared separately, retaining global illumination and occlusion. Full evidence, failures and exact hashes remain in the study folder; current R006 geometry/publication work belongs to the combined release owner.

### R006 major-edge correction and study04

A direct face/vertex audit found that several prominent new outer armor panels and ribs have **no bevel at all** (eight vertices, six faces, no modifier), unlike neighboring pieces with existing narrow bevels. The earlier 5–8 mm modifier summary did not describe these major surfaces. Study02/03 segment changes therefore could not soften them. [Study04](../assets/art-library/shipyard-hull/material-studies/native-r006-polymer-04/review.md) adds bounded front-perimeter geometry while preserving rear attachment corners and evaluated bounds; its actual runtime comparison remains pending. Approved sources and canonical outputs remain unchanged by this study.
