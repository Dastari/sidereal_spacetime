# Planet LOD authoring contract

Status: Required for new and revised planet integrations
Owner: Sidereal owner, 2026-09-11


Every new or revised planet renderer must account for transitions in seated Flight and Map → Observe, including a sudden jump to a distant body's close view. Follow R15 in [the rendering performance plan](rendering_performance_plan.md). LOD selection follows the render camera's projected body size, independently of ship position. Preserve authored surfaces, materials, body identity and authority state.

Generate terrain/composition, optical attributes and weather buffers in the planet worker. Build ahead before thresholds; retain the visible level until its replacement is uploaded and precompiled, then swap synchronously within one frame. Retain lower LOD nodes for revisits and share compatible materials/textures per body. Do not share distinct per-level/per-placement material state accidentally. Yield GPU preparation between frames, reject results for removed/revised bodies and release body-owned resources on disposal. Fixed-detail renderers must not rebuild merely because a LOD threshold changes.

Add NullEngine tests for swap ordering, retained levels, material/attribute preservation and disposal. Review approach, retreat and Map → Observe on real hardware with F3 open, recording build/pending counters and frame intervals; require no missing planet and no transition frame above20ms under the R15 acceptance setup. Save screenshots and JSON, including failures. Still images and geometry budgets alone do not establish transition performance; mark missing hardware verification explicitly. This requirement does not authorize visual redesign or waive art approval.

## Native material and geometry checks (2026-09-14)

When composing Blender meshes onto a curved globe, transport authored corner normals through the placement deformation and retain UV seams, material boundaries and triangle-to-placement ranges. A flat normal recomputed independently for every deformed triangle can introduce false diagonal facets across one authored planar surface. Check normal/UV parity against GLB exports, including hard edges, before changing the art to compensate for an importer fault.

Preserve complete glTF PBR behavior: linear material factors, sRGB color/emission textures, linear normal/roughness data, IOR, coat, alpha and double-sided lighting. `doubleSided` requires correct lighting on both faces as well as disabled back-face culling. Reuse compatible textures/materials across a body's retained levels; do not regenerate or recompile fixed ring/body materials at a geometry threshold. Validate recessed terrain with rays at every retained ground resolution so the substrate cannot cover basin, crater or glacial floors.

A visual working pass at one seed is not LOD acceptance. Review multiple seeds and angles, including reference-scale views, then validate the real Flight approach/retreat and Map → Observe path. Preserve failed source/export/capture revisions and separate importer diagnostics from artistic changes. Reference iteration evidence and current gates are recorded in [the 2026-09-14 iteration log](planet_reference_iteration_20260914.md).

When a visual defect persists after source edits, resolve the rendered triangle to its placement before further authoring. A visible ground fallback can cover or sit between the intended regional pieces; changing their hidden borders will not fix it. Native radial relief must survive composition rather than being normalized away, and its full height envelope must remain below recessed-floor clearance. Place secondary formations and weather against final overlapping terrain, including neighboring placements. Keep those spatial checks in worker/offline preparation, not the frame loop.

Export audits must match duplicate position/UV corners one-to-one when checking split normals. A greedy assignment can report false mismatches; verify exact matching at the original tolerances rather than loosening tolerances or modifying correct source shading. Append original editable Blender objects when reusing native geology. Corner-expanded JSON or GLB seams are not closed authoring topology; never weld unrelated pieces merely to bypass a manifold check.

For glass or transmissive ice, preserve native glTF transmission, IOR and volume attenuation separately from alpha. Keep snow caps and other opaque surfaces in their authored material roles. Share compatible glass materials across retained LODs, acquire the scene's refraction resource before shader preparation, and release it only after its final owner. Refraction adds an opaque-scene render pass: record its draw/submission cost and verify that disabled or pending LODs never appear in that pass. Test resource disposal and retained material/target identity; an attractive still image does not waive the transition budget.

Opaque native volumes must have coherent outward face orientation. Agreement between triangle winding and authored normals does not prove outward orientation when source faces use disconnected vertices. Check each intended closed panel/component independently, using diagnostic positional connectivity without merging unrelated pieces. Compare matched-camera native previews with back-face culling equivalent to glTF; Blender's two-sided viewport/Cycles rendering can conceal inward faces. If orientation is wrong, preserve the original triangle surfaces, UV corners and material assignments while correcting winding and signed normals in a new native revision. Do not hide the defect by making opaque materials double-sided or adding thickness.

Large native-kit tests must release parsed source fixtures between files. Run the reference check runner with one test subprocess at a time and a bounded heap; do not run full native validation alongside Blender generation or browser captures. A killed process with no result is an incomplete test run. Preserve its log and distinguish observed cumulative OOM counters from a proven process-specific failure.

Face-orientation repairs can cause Blender to choose different ngon diagonals. If preserving a previously exported surface requires a frozen triangle cache, store its source polygon/vertex/corner correspondence and validate it against the editable source, UVs, materials and signed normals. Record any native reevaluation difference explicitly; invalidate/rebuild the cache for subsequent geometry edits. A cached export must never silently diverge from its native source.
