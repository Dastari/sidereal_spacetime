# Tile plans, hull skins and sealed ship systems

Status: owner-approved design direction; implementation staged. 2026-09-08.

The detailed [Shipyard layout editor design](ship_layout_editor_design.md) and [external implementation prompt](handoffs/ship_layout_editor_implementation_prompt.md) turn this direction into an editor schema, workflows and staged acceptance gates.

The owner specified floor-plan tiles with automatic enclosing walls, separate external faction hull/armor skins and decals, internal rooms/doors/airlocks, pressure compartments, and an under-floor wire/pipe routing mode. This extends the existing authoritative refit direction; it is not a claim that pressure or utility simulation already exists.

Floor cells are stable authored placements with polygonal footprints: rectangles, triangles, trapezoids and compatible later shapes. Quantized vertices and matching edges form a continuous floor-plan union. Shared edges cancel; exposed boundary edges generate the pressure perimeter. Partial-edge junctions must split into matching segments before cancellation. Invalid overlaps, non-manifold joins, gaps and disconnected islands are reported explicitly. GPU batching may merge visible surfaces but never merges tile UUIDs, revision ownership, damage/collision state or construction selection identity.

There are distinct layers: floor/pressure structure; internal partitions and openings; external armor/hull skins; fitted equipment; service routes. Faction appearance belongs primarily to the external skin, trims and decal sockets. Changing skins must not alter the pressure boundary or silently move fittings. Internal partitions create rooms and doors create explicit connections between them. A rendered wall is not itself proof of a sealed compartment.

Pressure is future authoritative gameplay. Connected sealed volumes form zones; door/airlock state and breach openings determine gas exchange. An open connection can join pressure systems; a closed sealed door can isolate a breached compartment. Airlocks require their own validated interlocks and transition states. Hull punctures must update the actual pressure topology and contents through server rules, not merely hide a mesh. Oxygen, gas quantity, volume, exchange rates and environmental effects require their own schema, conservation tests and authority checks before this is described as implemented.

An under-floor routing mode exposes typed power/data/fluid ports and constrained wire/pipe paths. Reactors, drives, tanks and other equipment connect to networks through installed ports, not ownership or visual proximity. Server-validated route edits must check compatible medium/type, capacity, continuity, damage and access. Power/flow availability then feeds the existing flight-controller and equipment validators. Current authored fixture power/thrust availability is not such a network.

Normal gameplay selection should inspect useful equipment, engines and thrusters. Floor/structure tile selection belongs to construction, refit or explicit structural inspection mode. The earlier monolithic deck and newer individual Blender tiles are different presentation implementations, not competing authority models: construction needs stable tile addresses while the renderer should batch equivalent materials where practical. A room may be selected as a whole without discarding its constituent tile IDs.

Blender remains the editable visual source. Preserve polygon boundaries, openings, seal interfaces, major bevels and silhouette-changing detail as geometry. Use normal/bump, roughness, metallic and baked contact-detail maps for shallow seams, fasteners and panel relief where those do not change gameplay boundaries. Preserve GLB material roles and texture channels on export; validate tangents/normal-map response and useful mipmaps at gameplay scale. Texture detail does not replace collision, breach geometry, pressure seals or real large-form shadows. Shared material families, instancing and LOD reduce geometry/draw cost; increasing normal detail alone does not reduce the number of shadow passes.

Suggested implementation order: pure floor topology and snapping tests; draft editor and perimeter preview; validated revision-based blueprint/refit transactions; internal room/door topology; pressure simulation and airlock gameplay; typed utility routes and consumption. Preserve live item/fitting UUIDs, cargo and occupants during authoritative edits. No broad construction milestone is completed by this document.
