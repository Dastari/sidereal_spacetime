# Independent Astra — temperate scope and shadow provenance

Date: 2026-09-14. Re-viewed actual `temperate-baseline.png` and gas r005 corrected low-sun shadows-on/off pair.

## Temperate decision

The existing temperate baseline retains credible continent/water/forest/cliff identity and its historical interim acceptance is not revoked. However, the reference gap in this bounded pass is still open: uniformly tiled blue water lacks richer reflective cyan coast hierarchy; a flat connected land strip and fine uniform trees lack the reference's stronger continent/forest masses; repeated bead clouds remain conspicuous.

Recommend a bounded native temperate candidate reusing the working ocean r007 water/cloud/grove pipeline with explicit larger-continent and cliff composition. Two or three broader elevated landmasses and coherent forest groups should distinguish it from ocean archipelago recipes. A full independent new kit is unnecessary, but reusing ocean islands unchanged is insufficient. Preserve authored source surfaces and do not refine the legacy TypeScript voxel generator as the visual authoring source.

## Shadow provenance correction

Root reports an isolated-viewer filter emptied the shadow caster list while an LOD was disabled during staging, so many later candidate captures had no active shadow rendering despite the UI toggle. Prior working passes for desert r014, ocean r007 and gas r005 remain scoped to observed shape/color/material appearance only. They did not establish shadow, optical interaction or hardware timing acceptance.

The corrected gas low-sun pair shows visible shadow-dependent hatching on the body. A planet-cast dark sector on the ring remains visually unclear; the technical ring-shadow gate stays open. The pair is neither a pass nor proof of absent shadow behavior.

Corrected standard recaptures are required to ensure real shadows do not introduce appearance-breaking artifacts into the working candidates. No owner approval or production change is implied by this review.
