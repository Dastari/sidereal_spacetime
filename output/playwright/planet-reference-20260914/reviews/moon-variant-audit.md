## Implications for the current desert revision

Main-world desert r001 should explicitly cover `planets--desert-world` only unless cratered moon variants are actually authored and rendered. The two moon crops can later share rock/crater source geometry with rocky moons and use separately recorded pale/terracotta desert material variants. Their low-resolution appearance does not justify invisible fine-detail work or force monumental mesas onto them.

## LOD and coverage gates

Variants are authoring/composition distinctions, not new authority state or permission to manufacture live bodies. Retain stable crop IDs and exact recorded variant coverage. Sharing an authored kit does not confer visual acceptance on every recipe or family.

The coarse-scale identity visible in these tiny crops is the appropriate low-LOD target: a few dark crater openings, clear crust/interior division, sparse molten seams or major crystal protrusions. Preserve those landmarks through threshold changes while removing secondary detail. Test each distinct morphology through approach, retreat and sudden Map → Observe, using the existing worker/precompile/retention contract. A small source crop cannot prove hardware transition performance.

No moon has new agent acceptance from this reference-only audit. Fresh actual renders are required for each explicit variant before a shared main-world pass can claim to cover it.
